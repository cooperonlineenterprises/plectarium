# Licensing and Third-Party Material

No repository license has been selected by this foundation task. The packet
contains original specification text and references no copied third-party
implementation. Future dependencies, standards excerpts, schemas, generated
clients, icons, fonts, examples, containers, and datasets require provenance,
license compatibility, notice, and redistribution review before adoption.

Absence of a root license is intentional and must not be interpreted as a
license grant or a blocker silently resolved by a template.
