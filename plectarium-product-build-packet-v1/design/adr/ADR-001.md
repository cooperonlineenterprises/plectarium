# ADR-001: Plectarium identity and suite boundary

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decisions:** PLEC-001, PLEC-002, PLEC-003, PLEC-018

Plectarium is the formal suite product, part of the Octon ecosystem without an
Octon-prefixed name or dependency. It supplies a cohesive experience and
optional control plane while capabilities remain independently usable and
authoritative for their domains.

Rejected alternatives are an eleventh capability, a capability mega-engine,
an Octon-only interface, and hosted-only operation. These erase ownership and
create semantic/runtime coupling.
