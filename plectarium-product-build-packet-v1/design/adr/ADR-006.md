# ADR-006: Equivalent modes, tenant isolation, credential boundaries, and AI limits

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decisions:** PLEC-015, PLEC-016, PLEC-017, PLEC-020

Local, hosted, private-runner, customer-controlled, and air-gapped modes use
the same identity, authority, completion, and provenance contracts. Secret
values stay in credential brokers and runners redeem bounded opaque references.

Tenant isolation covers every control and data plane, including caches,
indexes, queues, runner leases, audit, and deduplication side channels. AI may
assist interpretation or experience but is not authority, the semantic engine,
a secret path, or a hidden executor. Structural validation remains non-product evidence.
