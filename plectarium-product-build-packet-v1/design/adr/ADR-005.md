# ADR-005: Immutable results, verification dimensions, and suite BOM

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decisions:** PLEC-012, PLEC-013, PLEC-014

Canonical bytes and records are content-addressed and immutable. Corrections,
withdrawals, trust changes, and purge states are successor/annotation records.
Checksum, signature, signer trust, schema validation, compatibility, freshness,
completion, and limitations remain independently visible.

Plectarium owns the exact supported suite BOM. The family defines contracts,
and the workspace may record tested checkouts, but neither creates the suite's
support promise.
