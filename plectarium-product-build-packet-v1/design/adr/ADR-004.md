# ADR-004: Plan-bound authority and truthful distributed lifecycle

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decisions:** PLEC-008, PLEC-009, PLEC-010, PLEC-011

The capability-authored plan is stored immutably and approval binds its exact
digest. Accepted permissions may narrow but never widen requested permissions.
Scheduling revalidates all bindings before a fenced attempt is leased.

Control state remains separate from capability completion. At-least-once queue
delivery, immutable attempts, fencing tokens, idempotency, and reconciliation
replace an unsupportable exactly-once claim. Cancellation requests remain
pending until acknowledgement or conclusive reconciliation.
