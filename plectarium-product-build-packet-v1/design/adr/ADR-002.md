# ADR-002: Immutable family source and multi-family coordinates

**Status:** Accepted  
**Decisions:** PLEC-004, PLEC-005

Plectarium consumes an external family by full Git commit, packet manifest and
checksums digests, and per-contract paths/digests. Catalog identity includes
family ID, capability ID, exact version, artifact digest, and contract identity.

Schema copies are rejected because they create a second mutable family
authority. Branch-only references and character/repository names are rejected
because they are mutable or ambiguous. During prepublication work, unresolved
pin fields remained explicit rather than receiving guessed values. They are now
resolved to the published family closure commit and verified contract bytes.
