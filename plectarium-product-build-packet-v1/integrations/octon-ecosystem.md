# Octon Ecosystem Integration

Octon may discover exact Plectarium/capability identities, route an authorized
request, inspect plan/effects, invoke through a narrow adapter, and store durable
result references. Plectarium does not import Octon internal state or assume
Octon is present.

Octon continues to own its trust, decisions, downstream tasks, handoff, and
action authority. A Plectarium job or capability result cannot create or close
Octon work unless a separately authorized Octon process does so.
