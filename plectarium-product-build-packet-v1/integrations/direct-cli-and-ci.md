# Direct Capability CLI and CI

Plectarium support must not become a mandatory runtime. A capability's
canonical CLI and CI integration continue to discover, plan, execute, inspect,
and emit durable results independently.

The suite CLI may coordinate those same contracts locally. CI remains owner of
checkout, credentials, scheduling, cache/artifact infrastructure, and policy
enforcement. Plectarium supplies no implicit secret, network, installation, or
downstream deployment authority.
