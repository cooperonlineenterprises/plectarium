# Plectarium Decision Index

The machine-readable authority is `design/decisions.yaml`; detailed rationale
is in `design/decision-ledger.md` and `design/adr/`.

Established decisions come directly from the current portfolio authority or
binding family invariants. Accepted decisions resolve Plectarium-specific
architecture. Deferred and open items remain non-claims.

| Range | Subject |
|---|---|
| PLEC-001–004 | identity, source ownership, capability boundary |
| PLEC-005–010 | modular architecture, plans, authority, job semantics |
| PLEC-011–016 | artifacts, compatibility, modes, credentials, tenancy |
| PLEC-017–020 | direct use, AI limits, extraction, readiness truth |
