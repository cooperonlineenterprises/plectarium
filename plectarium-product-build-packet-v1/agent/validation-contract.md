# Task Validation Contract

Implementation tasks must produce:

- exact subject revision and environment;
- positive, negative, mutation, and failure-path results appropriate to risk;
- schema/API/event/storage compatibility evidence;
- authority, tenancy, completion, and no-domain-semantics evidence;
- changed paths and external effects;
- skipped checks, limitations, expiry, and reassessment triggers;
- claim/release-gate links.

Text existence, mock-only success, a single transport, or a happy-path demo
cannot satisfy a product claim. High-risk tasks require independent review or
an explicitly documented limitation when independent review is unavailable.
