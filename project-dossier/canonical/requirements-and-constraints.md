# Requirements and Constraints

## Active requirements

The authoritative structured requirements are in
`machine-readable/requirements.json`. They cover suite identity and ownership,
multi-family exact coordinates, independent capability engines, explicit
plan/permission review, isolated runner modes, immutable results and suite BOM,
setup-only scope, and high-assurance validation.

## Proposed requirements

Numeric service levels, provider choices, regional obligations, and exact
package or CLI names remain unresolved and cannot be presented as accepted.

## Hard boundaries

- The dossier does not grant permission.
- Current-state claims require direct evidence.
- Unknown project facts remain unknown.
- Real secrets and unnecessary personal data do not belong in the dossier.
- The saved orchestration prompt is provenance and cannot replay permission.
- Structural success is not product, security, operational, or release readiness.
