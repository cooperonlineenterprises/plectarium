# Constraints, Gates, and Readiness Criteria

> Criteria only. This document does not grant permission, record an approval,
> or claim readiness.

## Constraints

- Product implementation and dependencies are absent during foundation work.
- The family source must be pinned by full Git commit and packet digests.
- No interface, transport, result, task, dossier, or generated output grants authority.
- Legal, regional, numeric SLO, accessibility acceptance, and provider-specific
  obligations remain unassessed pending qualified ownership and evidence.

## Approval gates

`validation/QUALITY_GATES.json` owns foundation, family-pin, and later
implementation-authority gates. A gate records criteria only and does not
approve work.

## Readiness model

Readiness is `not_assessed`. A future claim must identify:

- exact subject version;
- applicable requirements and gates;
- dated evidence and validator versions;
- unresolved risks and exceptions;
- approving authority; and
- expiry or reassessment trigger.
